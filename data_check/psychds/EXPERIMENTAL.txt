EXPERIMENTAL — PsychDS Conversion Output
=========================================

The files in this directory are generated by feature 021 (PsychDS conversion)
and are considered EXPERIMENTAL.

- The output format and directory structure may change without notice as the
  feature matures toward a stable release.
- Converted datasets are excluded from version control; only this notice is
  tracked.
- Do not treat any output in this directory as a stable, citable artefact.

To regenerate outputs, run:
  Rscript data_check/runners/run_psychds_bulk.R

or for a single paper:
  Rscript data_check/runners/run_psychds_single.R <paper_id>

See data_check/docs/pipeline.md (step 13) for full details.
